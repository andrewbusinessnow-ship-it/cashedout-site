Support

Need help right now? You don't have to get through this alone. These are free and confidential:

  • US, gambling: National Problem Gambling Helpline, call or text 1-800-MY-RESET (1-800-697-3738), 24/7.
  • US, crisis or thinking about suicide: call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), 24/7.
  • UK: National Gambling Helpline 0808 8020 133; Samaritans 116 123, 24/7.
  • Australia: Gambling Help 1800 858 858; Lifeline 13 11 14, 24/7.
  • Anywhere else: findahelpline.com lists free helplines in your country. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Cashed Out is a self-help tool, not treatment or a substitute for professional help.

Contact us

Email andrewbusinessnow@gmail.com. We usually reply within two business days. We can't give crisis support by email; if you need help now, use the numbers above.

Common questions

I'm having an urge right now

Open the app and tap the red Panic Button on Home (or Having an urge? if you see the subscription screen). It's free and starts a short breathing exercise straight away.

I bet again. What now?

On Home, tap Reset. Your new streak starts, and every clean day you've had stays on your record. If it's been a hard night, the helplines are on that screen too.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Cashed Out → Cancel Subscription, or tap Manage subscription in the app's Profile tab. Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date. You keep access until the paid period ends.

I already paid. How do I get back in?

Open the app and tap Restore purchase on the welcome or subscription screen, signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where is my data, and how do I delete it?

Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. To erase it, use Profile → Reset all data, or delete the app. See the Privacy Policy.

I'm behind on bills or in debt because of gambling

You're not alone, and it can be sorted out step by step. The app's Library has Debt and money help. In the US, free nonprofit credit counselling is available through the NFCC at nfcc.org or 1-800-388-2227, and the gambling helpline can help with money worries too.
