Privacy Policy

Cashed Out · Lone Star Horns LLC ("we", "us") · Effective November 16, 2026

The short version. There's no account and no sign-in. Your answers, streaks, check-ins, journal and everything else you put into Cashed Out stay on your iPhone. We don't run a server that receives your data, we don't show ads, we don't track you across apps, and we don't sell anything about you. Apple handles all payments.

1. What stays on your iPhone

The app stores these only in its own storage on your device. We never receive them:

  • Your setup answers: optional first name, how long and how often you've bet, your weekly spend estimate and the money estimate made from it, your goals and personal reason, the quiz answers you choose to give, and your quit date.
  • Your recovery record: clean streaks and resets (with any note you add), daily check-ins (mood, urge level, triggers, whether you bet, and any note), pledges, Panic-button sessions (urge level and how it went), calm-game sessions (when you answer "How's the urge now?" after a game: the time, your urge level and how it went; which game you played and what you did in it aren't saved), badges, and your journal entries.
  • Trusted contacts: if you pick up to three people to text when an urge hits, their names and phone numbers. The app only gets the contacts you pick in Apple's contact picker; it can't see the rest of your address book.
  • Settings: reminder on/off and time, which Library articles you've read, whether we've already asked for an App Store rating this version, and a saved copy of your subscription status so the app opens offline.

Cashed Out doesn't sync to iCloud or to us. If you have iPhone backups turned on (iCloud Backup or a computer), iOS may include this data in your backup, as it does for any app.

2. What leaves your iPhone, and who gets it

Apple

Subscriptions are bought and managed through Apple's App Store. Apple processes your payment; we never see your name, email, card or Apple Account details. The app asks Apple, on your device, whether your subscription is active. If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash and usage statistics. When the app asks for a rating, Apple shows and handles that prompt. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Things you choose to open or send

  • Calls and texts: helpline, crisis-line and trusted-contact buttons open your Phone or Messages app. A message to a trusted contact is only drafted; nothing is sent unless you press send.
  • Websites: links (helplines, help directories, these pages) open in your browser.
  • Share card: "The house lost $X to me." is made on your iPhone and goes only where you share it. If you choose to save it, iOS asks permission to add it to Photos; the app can't read your photos.
  • Email to us: if you email support, we receive what you send and your email address, and use it only to reply.

3. Notifications

Reminders and milestone notes are local notifications: your iPhone schedules them itself, with no server involved. Their text never mentions betting. Turn them off in the app (Profile → Reminders) or in iOS Settings.

4. What we don't do

  • No account, no sign-in, no ads, no advertising identifier, no tracking across apps.
  • No access to your location, camera, microphone, health data, photos (other than adding a card you save) or full contacts.
  • No connection to your bank, cards or betting accounts.
  • We don't sell or rent your data.

5. Deleting your data

Use Profile → Reset all data in the app, or delete the app. Both erase everything listed in section 1 from your iPhone (backups made earlier keep their own copy until they're replaced). Because we don't hold a copy, we can't recover data you delete, and there's nothing on our side to delete. Deleting the app doesn't cancel a subscription; see the Terms.

6. Children and teens

Cashed Out isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. The app is designed for adults; if you're under 18, the free help in the app (helplines and breathing) is there for you too.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here with a new effective date before the change applies.
